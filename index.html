About CSAC

Building leaders of tomorrow, guided by values and excellence

Club Establishment & History: Civil Services Aspirants Club (CSAC) was established in 2014 by Rahul Javir Sir (IAS, Assam cadre), an esteemed alumnus of GCOEARA, to guide students towards administrative excellence. The club was revived in 2021 by alumni Devashish Chhatre and Pratik Bhosale, continuing its legacy of peer support.

Vision: To nurture integrity, leadership, and analytical excellence in young engineering graduates, shaping them into empathetic and dedicated civil servants who will serve the nation with distinction.

Mission: To provide top-tier mentorship, resources, structured guidance, and peer-to-peer learning environments, preparing aspirants to clear competitive examinations with confidence.

Guidance by Alumni Officers: CSAC stands proud with a robust support network of more than 40 distinguished officers from various departments who guide current students through webinars, strategy sessions, and mocks.

Examinations We Cover

Comprehensive preparation pathways for administrative and military leadership

Civil Services & Administration Wing

Shaping governance, policy, and public administration to serve the republic.

UPSC CSE

योगः कर्मसु कौशलम्

Union Public Service Commission Civil Services Exam for IAS, IPS, IFS, and Central Services.

MPSC State Services

मर्यादा रक्षितुं लोकान्

Maharashtra Public Service Commission exams for Deputy Collector, DYSP, Tehsildar, and state administration.

SSC CGL

निष्पक्षता एवं सत्यनिष्ठा

Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level exam for key inspector and assistant roles in ministries.

Banking Exams

आर्थिक सुरक्षा

IBPS PO, Clerk, Specialist Officers, SBI PO, and RBI Grade B officer examinations.

Railways (RRB)

राष्ट्र की जीवन रेखा

Railway Recruitment Board NTPC, Junior Engineer (JE), and senior section engineer examinations.

National Armed Forces Wing

Guardian of sovereignty, territorial integrity, and honor on land, air, and sea.

UPSC CDS

सेवा परमो धर्मः

Combined Defence Services Exam to enter Indian Military Academy, Naval Academy, Air Force Academy, & OTA.

UPSC NDA

सेवा परमो धर्मः

National Defence Academy entrance exam for entry into army, navy, and airforce wings after 12th/early college.

AFCAT

नभः स्पृशं दीप्तम्

Air Force Common Admission Test for flying branch, technical and non-technical ground duties.

UPSC CAPF

राष्ट्र रक्षा समो धर्मः

Central Armed Police Forces Assistant Commandants exam for BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB.
